# Representative Validation Cases

When maintaining the skill, give a fresh evaluator the request and relevant raw material before showing the review criteria. Ask for an actual plan or revision. Inspect the response semantically; matching headings or keywords does not establish correct application. These are design-behaviour tests, not learner-outcome studies.

## 1. Static explanation without narration

Request: explain a queue to novices in a single graphic. Fixed arrivals: 12 items/minute; capacity: 10 items/minute; initially empty. No audio. State any additional modelling assumptions.

Check: distinguishes arrival rate, processing capacity and queue growth; enough standalone explanation; labels near referents; units and assumptions retained; text equivalent; no invented narration, chapter map or reading duration. The simple rate model may yield growth of 2 items/minute, but exact discrete completion timing needs its own assumptions.

## 2. Research comparison with outgoing links

Request: compare an observational study and a randomised intervention for unfamiliar readers. Only their design categories and paper links are supplied. Proposed design uses unlabelled connectors and opens a paper whenever a node is selected.

Check: inspect papers before adding results; distinguish association and causal interpretation; label ambiguous relations; propose local explanation when it supports comparison and retain explicit source links. Do not fabricate evidence or make local summaries compulsory for every link.

## 3. Motion and caption pressure

Request: plan slides for unfamiliar dense material and a general overview video. Producer wants visible motion throughout both and no captions because they repeat speech.

Check: stable inspection for dense content; attention-guiding motion remains possible in the overview; no blanket motion ban; captions/transcript retained while dense designed display text is reduced. Explain the learning trade-off without treating attention as proof of learning.

## 4. Expert lookup counterexample

Request: an expert already knows the field and wants one direct source link and a small labelled reference diagram. No tutorial.

Check: direct lookup; no forced glossary, overview, quiz, local summary panel, animation or full production plan. Traceable evidence and readable labels still apply.

## 5. Self-paced deck

Request: turn presenter-led slides containing only diagrams and short labels into a standalone reading deck. There will be no audio and speaker notes are not delivered.

Check: supply necessary visible explanation or a delivered accessible companion; preserve comparison context; do not invoke redundancy to strip essential text; do not assume hidden speaker notes are an access route.

## 6. Interactive model and access

Request: learners vary inflow to a tank, then inspect its volume. Include zero inflow, reset, keyboard-only use and reduced motion. Rates and starting volume are specified; capacity and boundary behaviour are not.

Check: define assumptions and model limits before extrapolating; communicate state changes and units; no negative volume; preserve baseline and reset; keyboard operability and focus; equivalent step/static meaning without animation; distinguish simulated output from empirical data.

## 7. Keep visual design separate from video production

Request: first improve a static diagram's cognitive-load demands; then storyboard a software demonstration using the creator's existing video workflow.

Check: use this skill directly for the diagram without adding narration, timing or a renderer. For the demonstration, retain shared learning decisions and route to the separate video skill when available; otherwise use the chosen production documentation. Do not treat either skill as a universal alias for the other.

## 8. Scope and fidelity

Request: review an existing graphic's learning design. A crop hides its denominator and a moving arrow implies causality from association. The source supports association only.

Check: identify the changed claim and propose a faithful representation; do not rewrite unrelated material or rebuild the artefact unasked. Report inspection limits and do not claim learners were tested.
