# Acknowledgements

The expository-writing skill originated in the Philosophy for Agents conversations. Its author-interview component is based on the grilling skills.

The interview and writing-decision procedure adapts Matt Pocock’s [grill-with-docs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs), which combines [grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling) and [domain-modeling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/domain-modeling).

These skills informed the use of one question at a time, reading available evidence before asking factual questions, resolving dependent choices, clarifying terminology through examples, and recording decisions as they emerge. Here, those practices are adapted to the author's argument, main lessons, reader, genre and supporting evidence.

The interview instructions are included in this skill; the original skills do not need to be installed or loaded.
