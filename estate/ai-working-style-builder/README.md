# AI Working Style Builder

*Teach AI how you work.*

AI Working Style Builder helps with two related problems:

- **how AI communicates with you** — structure, length, clarity, pacing, and useful formats;
- **how work survives the conversation** — decisions, plans, notes, and things you want to return to.

## Who this is for

You do not need to know exactly what is wrong before you begin. You may have noticed that:

- long answers make the main point difficult to find;
- detailed instructions are useful but tiring to follow;
- step-by-step help becomes tedious;
- conversations wander and the original task disappears;
- decisions or useful context become difficult to retrieve;
- you keep repeating information across chats;
- an output is hard to review, reuse, or share.

You may have found this skill yourself, received the link from someone else, installed it in a desktop agent, or pasted its prompt into an ordinary chatbot. You may want one quick improvement or an evolving working system. The process should adapt to that situation rather than assume everyone needs the same setup.

Accessibility and neurodivergence may be relevant, but the skill starts from concrete problems rather than diagnostic labels.

## How the process works

The skill follows a few principles:

- **Design for variable attention by default:** It does not wait for you to identify overload, distraction, likely interruption, or a preferred learning style before providing accessible structure and alternative depths.
- **Understand before configuring:** It learns what brought you here and what work you want to improve before drafting durable instructions.
- **Accept an incomplete account:** You can dictate naturally, mention several connected problems, or change your mind later.
- **Show an example early:** Once a likely pattern appears, the assistant demonstrates one small contrast rather than delaying all value until the interview is complete.
- **Keep examples bounded:** It normally compares two compact versions, not a large catalogue that creates more overload.
- **Make one decision at a time:** Questions stay focused, while facts already available in your account or files are not asked again.
- **Test preference against usefulness:** What looks appealing and what helps you find or act on information may differ.
- **Deliver before exhaustive discovery:** You should normally see a useful experiment or starter result within two or three exchanges.
- **Preserve revision history:** New preferences begin as experiments. You can backtrack, refine them, and keep the reasons for changes.

## Start with an installed skill

In Codex, Claude Code, or another agent with this skill installed, invoke **AI Working Style Builder** and explain that you want to improve how you work with AI or with that assistant. Add whatever you already know about the problem; you do not need to use exact wording.

The agent loads [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md), then only the supporting references relevant to your situation.

## Use it in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini

Paste the prompt below into a new conversation. It works without installing a skill. If the chatbot can open links, it can also consult the [browser guide](https://techczech.github.io/dominiks-agent-skills/estate/ai-working-style-builder/assets/ai-working-style-builder-guide.html) or the [public skill source](https://github.com/techczech/dominiks-agent-skills/tree/main/estate/ai-working-style-builder).

```text
I'd like to improve how I work with AI and how I work with you.

Help me discover what would make the biggest difference. First understand what brought me here, what I am trying to do, and whether I want a quick improvement or a more durable setup. Invite me to describe or dictate several connected problems without organising them first. If I have already explained the problem, work from that account instead of asking me to repeat it.

Treat variable attention, knowledge, reading depth, and interruption as normal. Do not wait for me to disclose overload, distraction, disability, likely interruption, or a preferred learning style before using accessible structure. For substantive explanations or multi-stage instructions, answer the immediate question first. Make a quick route, guided route, uninterrupted complete route, optional technical depth, and resumable version available. Expose these routes without making me answer repeated permission questions or justify why I want one of them.

Reflect the problem in plain language. Consider both how information is presented and whether decisions, plans, or context are being lost in chat. Do not diagnose me or assume that my first stated preference is necessarily what works best.

Give me a small example sooner rather than later. Once you see a plausible pattern, explain the principle briefly and compare two compact generic examples. Do not overwhelm me with a catalogue. When useful, offer to test the same principle on my content. Distinguish what I like aesthetically from what helps me find, understand, or act on information.

Do not turn this into a long questionnaire. Ask one decision question at a time, prefer one substantial dictation to many small questions, and accept backtracking. Within two or three exchanges, offer a useful result, a starter version, or the choice to continue refining.

Possible results include editable custom instructions, a communication experiment, a working-style profile, or a simple way to preserve decisions and resume work. Treat new preferences as experiments first. Let me review any custom instructions or proposed memory before adopting them.

If lost history matters, explain the principle that chat supports exploration but important decisions, plans, instructions, and lessons need a durable home. Recommend the smallest useful journal or resumable document. Mention disk-based desktop agents as the stronger option for repeated work while accommodating ordinary chatbot use.

Keep output human-readable. Offer Markdown or a journal entry as an editable canonical source and, when useful, HTML as a separate reading surface. A reading HTML version may provide quick, guided, complete, and technical-depth routes through the same content. Offer feedback and sharing versions later instead of combining those purposes by default.

Full guide, if you can open links:
https://techczech.github.io/dominiks-agent-skills/estate/ai-working-style-builder/assets/ai-working-style-builder-guide.html
```

Add your own account below the prompt or send it in the next message. Dictation is often easier than formulating the problem precisely.

## What the journey looks like

1. **You initiate it:** You ask to improve how you work with AI or describe a specific difficulty.
2. **The assistant understands your situation:** It identifies your purpose, environment, immediate problem, and desired scale of help.
3. **You see a small example:** The assistant demonstrates one likely useful principle without waiting for a complete profile.
4. **You try a result:** This may be a reformatted example, communication rule, custom instruction, or resumption note.
5. **You choose the depth:** Stop with the quick result or continue into durable instructions and records.
6. **You refine through use:** Keep what works, revise what does not, and preserve the reasons for changes.

The skill may be used once or twice to produce a practical result. It can also be revisited after real work reveals new needs.

## The central information-management principle

Clearer responses solve only part of the problem. Chat is useful for exploration and feedback, but important decisions, plans, instructions, lessons, and things to return to need a durable home when they must survive the conversation.

A desktop agent with access to files can maintain that history most reliably. Custom instructions, chatbot memory, and saved resumption documents remain useful fallbacks.
