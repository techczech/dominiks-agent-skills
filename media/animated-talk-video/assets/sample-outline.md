# How a recipe becomes a loaf

## The idea

### Why bread rises {statement}
- Yeast eats sugar and breathes out gas
> Bread rises because yeast eats the sugar in the flour and breathes out gas. The gas gets trapped in the dough.

### What the dough needs {iconlist}
- Flour {icon=lucide:wheat}
- Water {icon=lucide:droplet}
- Yeast {icon=lucide:sprout}
- Time {icon=lucide:timer}
> The dough needs four things. Flour. Water. Yeast. And time, which is the one people forget.

### From dough to loaf {process}
- Mix {icon=lucide:utensils}
- Rest {icon=lucide:bed}
- Shape {icon=lucide:hand}
- Bake {icon=lucide:flame}
> You mix the dough, then you let it rest. After that you shape it, and finally you bake it.
