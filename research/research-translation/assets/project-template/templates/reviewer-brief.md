## What To Review

Please review the translated text for meaning, clarity, naturalness, and suitability for the intended reader group.

## What To Flag

- unclear or unnatural wording
- meaning changes
- missing information
- tone that feels coercive, too formal, too casual, or inappropriate
- terminology that may confuse participants
- questionnaire items or response options that do not preserve the source meaning

## What Not To Review

Do not rewrite everything for style unless the current wording creates a real problem.
