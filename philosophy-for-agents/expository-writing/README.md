# Expository writing

This skill helps you develop an explanation or argument with an AI writing agent. It starts with what you want to say, who will read it and what supports your claims. You can inspect the planning, drafting and review records as the work develops.

## What to expect

For a new piece, the agent reads your materials and interviews you about your intended argument, main lessons, audience and destination. It asks one question at a time and records your answers. You can say **“skip the grilling”** or continue from decisions you have already settled.

Before drafting, you review the purpose, claims and outline. The agent then works section by section, with a separate critic checking the argument, evidence, wording and connections between paragraphs. When the explanation needs a workflow, table, screenshot or appendix, those are planned and reviewed with the text.

Your wording, edits and deletions remain authoritative. If you change the direction, the agent updates the decision records and identifies the plans and drafts affected. A small correction does not restart the whole process.

## How to use it

Ask your agent to use **expository-writing** and provide your source materials, existing draft or initial idea. Tell it which passages must be preserved and what you want to work on first. For example:

> Use expository-writing to help me develop this paper. Read the draft and source records, then interview me about the argument before proposing an outline. Preserve my opening paragraph.

The process leaves editable declarations, claim records, outlines, drafts, reviews and decisions in your existing project folder. You can also request a self-contained HTML inspector to explore what each stage produced and what informed it.
